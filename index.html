          When the week goes sideways

          Only 3, 2 or 1 days this week

          3 days: Day 1, Day 2, Day 4. Add face pulls and calf raises to Day 2. Runs: the long run and one easy run.

          2 days: A = goblet squat, dumbbell bench, lat pulldown, RDL, farmer’s carry, dead bug. B = split squat, row, seated press, hip thrust, push-ups, side plank and a hang. Keep the long run.

          1 day: 45 minutes of goblet squat, RDL, push-up, cable row, farmer’s carry (3 sets each), plank (2). Long run if you can.

          0 days: 8,000 steps a day. Next week starts at the same weights.

          Bad night, or an on-call night

          Under 5 hours, or called in overnight: 2 sets of everything at the same weights. Walk instead of running.

          5 to 6 hours: train as planned, but no weight increases and no strides.

          On-call day: train if you haven’t been called in by 5 PM. If you are, move the session to the next free day and keep the order.

          Sleeping heart rate 5+ above normal for 3 days: make this a lighter week.

          Something hurts
          • Dull, both sides, a day or two after training. Normal soreness. Train, lighter if it’s bad.
          • Sharp, in a joint, on one movement. Swap that exercise. Still there next session: tell Claude.
          • Shooting, tingling, numb, or back pain going down a leg. Stop that movement today and tell Claude.
          • Chest pain, palpitations, odd breathlessness, dizziness. Stop right now. Charaka, not training.
          Ill, travelling, or back after a break

          Cold above the neck, no fever: a walk or a half session.

          Fever or body aches: rest until 1 to 2 days symptom-free, then two sessions at about two-thirds.

          Travel week: 30 minutes, three times: split squats, push-ups, one-leg hinge, backpack rows, one-leg glute bridge, plank, side plank, one-leg calf raise. Plus two easy 30-minute runs or walks.

          Back after 2 weeks off: about 90% of your weights for a week. 3+ weeks: about 80%, building back over 2 to 3 weeks; runs drop back two weeks.

          What changed in v1.1

          Added for flexibility, mobility and stamina. Strength days, runs, dates and rules are unchanged.

          • Warm-up, 12 min: adds wrist rocks, ankle rocks (every gym day, not just Day 4), open books, 90/90 hip switches, lateral lunges and a 20-s deep squat hold. Ankles, hips, mid-back and wrists were only worked on the optional Day 5.
          • Mobility in the rests: one stretch between sets of each day’s first two lifts. Costs no time, since the rests are mandatory anyway.
          • Full range on every rep. Strength work through full range improves flexibility about as much as stretching does.
          • Cool-down, 8 min: hip flexors and hamstrings twice, an adductor stretch, and 1 min of belly breathing. Lasting range needs roughly 5+ minutes of stretching per muscle a week; desk days tighten exactly these.
          • Mobility flow, 15 min on the weekly rest day, after the walk.
          • Day 5: gets back the mobility block from the full plan (this page had dropped it), now 10 min.
          • Stamina finisher on Day 2 in weeks 7–9 and 11–12, only after clear screening labs: 3 moderate rounds, 8 min. Day 2 is the one gym day without a run.
          • Strides also in week 11, same rule as weeks 7 and 9.
          • CPR drill rounds spelled out: 2 in weeks 1–4 and 10, 3 in weeks 5–8, 4 in weeks 9, 11 and 12.
          • New tests: ankle, toe touch, deep squat and overhead reach (Thu 12 Nov), and minutes run without walking plus average heart rate (Sun 15 Nov). Both retests repeat them.

          Never

          • Train to failure. Stop with 2 or 3 reps left.
          • Let an easy run go above about 135 bpm. Walk until it drops.
          • Put Day 4 the day before the long run.
          • Go past 70 minutes in the gym.
